Hey! I'm **Cipta Fikri Wiratama**, known online as **fikriw** — a
caffeine-powered software engineer building code into stories. I'm a
4th-year Informatics student at Universitas Diponegoro, working as a
full-stack and mobile developer and dabbling in AI/ML research on the
side.

Dart), and AI/ML (TensorFlow, LangChain, RAG pipelines) — most recently

70–80% of repetitive queries autonomously.

I care a lot about turning rough ideas into production-grade systems:
clean architecture, resilient infrastructure, and interfaces people
actually enjoy using. Along the way I've picked up 44 Google Cloud skill
badges and a few other certifications — you can see those on the
[certificates](/certificates/) page, project awards and highlights on
[achievements](/achievements/), and what I've shipped on the
[projects](/projects/) page.

Outside of programming, I enjoy reading scientific health stuff backed up
