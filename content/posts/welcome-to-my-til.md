This is my personal "Today I Learned" log — short notes on things I pick up
while studying, debugging, or just poking around.

To add a new note, drop a new Markdown file into `content/posts/`, like this
one, with front matter for `title`, `date`, and `tags`. No template changes
needed — once the note is pushed to GitHub, Vercel rebuilds the site with
Hugo and the home, list, and tag pages pick it up automatically.
